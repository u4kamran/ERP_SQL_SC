Private Sub Form_KeyUp(Keycode As Integer, Shift As Integer)

On Error GoTo TrapError

    Call EnterKeyEnable(Keycode)

Exit Sub

TrapError:

    Call ShowError(lngError, Me.Caption)

End Sub
